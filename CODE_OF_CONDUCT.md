# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and
harassment-free environment for contributors, submitters, curators, and
community members.

## Expected behaviour

Participants should:

- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Give constructive and specific feedback.
- Respect differences in experience, discipline, background, and viewpoint.
- Focus criticism on ideas, records, metadata, documentation, or code rather
  than individuals.
- Protect confidential and sensitive information.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Follow applicable legal, ethical, and institutional requirements.
- Accept curator decisions professionally, including requests for changes or
  decisions to decline submissions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Threats or incitement
- Deliberate disruption
- Publication of private information without permission
- Repeated unwanted contact
- Spam or advertising
- Fraudulent authorship or identity claims
- Deliberate submission of malicious files
- Sharing credentials or restricted data
- Retaliation against people who report concerns

## Scope

This code applies to:

- GitHub issues
- Pull requests
- Discussions
- Repository contributions
- Community curation interactions
- Other official community communication channels

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates this code.

Depending on severity and context, maintainers may:

- Issue a warning
- Request corrective action
- Close or lock an issue
- Decline a submission
- Remove a contributor from community spaces
- Report serious conduct to the relevant platform or institution

## Reporting

Do not report private, sensitive, or security-related concerns in a public
issue.

Use GitHub private vulnerability reporting for security concerns when
available:

https://github.com/rotsl/biologyautomation/security/advisories/new

For other sensitive conduct concerns, contact a repository maintainer through
an appropriate private channel listed on their GitHub profile.

## Good-faith reporting

Retaliation against a person making a good-faith report is prohibited.

Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves be treated as a violation.
